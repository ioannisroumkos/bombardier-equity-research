# Bombardier Equity Research Report

## About the Project

This project was an attempt to take the concepts and techniques I was learning through my educational journey and apply them to a real-world case.

I chose Bombardier because I genuinely admire the company and was interested in its recent turnaround and focus on business aviation. Rather than keeping what I was learning purely theoretical, I wanted to see how far I could take it by researching a real company, building my own assumptions and forecasts, and applying financial modeling and valuation techniques to an actual case.

The result is my first full equity research report, covering Bombardier's business and industry, financial performance, forecasts, valuation, scenario analysis, and key risks. More than anything, the project was an opportunity to **learn by doing** and better understand how financial concepts translate into real-world decisions and analysis.

This project is an independently prepared equity research report on Bombardier Inc. (TSX: BBD.B). The report develops a SELL / UNDERWEIGHT thesis using fundamental analysis, operating forecasts, discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation, comparable-company analysis, scenario analysis, and sensitivity testing.

## What the Project Covers

The report combines qualitative research with a structured financial model. The main areas I worked on were:

- **Company & industry research:** Bombardier's business model, business segments, competitive positioning, aftermarket services, industry demand, and supply-chain dynamics.
- **Financial analysis & forecasting:** historical financial performance and five-year forecasts covering revenue, aircraft deliveries, services growth, operating costs, working capital, CapEx, taxes, debt, and cash generation.
- **Valuation:** a DCF using a 9.8% WACC and 2.0% terminal growth, complemented by comparable-company analysis using EV/EBITDA.
- **Scenario & sensitivity analysis:** Best Case, Base Case, and Worst Case scenarios, together with a 5×5 WACC / terminal-growth sensitivity analysis.
- **Investment thesis:** bringing the analysis together to assess the main catalysts, risks, and the gap between modeled intrinsic value and the market price used in the report.

The report reaches a **SELL / UNDERWEIGHT** conclusion for Bombardier. The Base Case DCF produces a target price of **C$227.09 per share**, compared with the **C$352.72** reference market price used in the analysis.

A central part of the thesis is that Bombardier's operational improvement and deleveraging are genuine positives, but the valuation implied by the market price leaves limited room for execution problems, weaker demand, supply-chain disruption, or weaker-than-expected cash generation.

Overall, the analysis suggests that, under the assumptions used in the model, Bombardier's operational improvements and deleveraging do not fully support the valuation implied by the reference market price.
